Questions

Is Vortexia really free?

Yes. Music, moderation, tickets, economy and levels are free, with no paywall on core features.

How do I add it to my server?

Press Add to Discord, pick your server, and approve the permissions. You need Manage Server on that server.

Does music work 24/7?

Yes. Keep Vortexia in your voice channel with 24/7 mode, powered by Lavalink.

Slash or prefix, which should I use?

Both work. Core features use slash commands, and quick fun and utility commands use the ? prefix, like ?meme or ?rank.

How do tickets work?

Post a support panel, members open a private ticket channel, and staff claim and close it.

Can I hide my server or profile from this site?

Yes. Ask in the support server and your server or profile is removed from the showcase.

Where can I get help?

Join the support server and ask. Someone will be around.
