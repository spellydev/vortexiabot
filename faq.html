FAQ

STRAIGHT
ANSWERS.

Common questions about Vortexia, invites, music and features.

Is Vortexia really free?

Yes. Invite and use the core modules — music, moderation, tickets, economy, levels and utility — with no premium paywall required.

How do I add Vortexia to my server?

Click any Add to Discord button on this site, choose your server, authorize the permissions, then type / in a channel to see commands.

Does music work 24/7?

Yes. Enable 24/7 mode from the music panel so Vortexia stays in the voice channel even when the queue is empty or members leave.

What can the music bot play?

Lavalink-backed playback supports common sources (including YouTube and search queries). Open /music after joining a voice channel.

How do tickets work?

Run /ticket setup to post a panel. Members click a button to open a private ticket channel; staff can add users and close tickets when done.

Where can I get help?

Join the official support server: discord.gg/7RfWAKeAZZ

Is Vortexia affiliated with Discord?

No. Vortexia is an independent bot and is not affiliated with Discord Inc.

Still stuck?

Ask in the support server — the team and community can help.
