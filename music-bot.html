Vortexia Bot includes a Lavalink-powered music player with a control panel, queue, filters, lyrics and 24/7 mode — free.

A Discord music bot that stays stable

Vortexia's music module uses Lavalink for streaming. Join a voice channel, run /music, and use the player panel to play, pause, skip, change volume and view the queue.

Music features

  • Player control panel opened with /music
  • Queue with /queue and search/play with /play
  • Audio filters and loop modes
  • Lyrics with /lyrics
  • 24/7 voice mode with /247 so the bot stays in the channel

Set up music in 3 steps

  1. Add Vortexia Bot to your server.
  2. Join a voice channel.
  3. Run /music or /play and enjoy. Turn on /247 for round-the-clock presence.

FAQ

Is the Vortexia music bot free?

Yes, the music module is part of the free core features.

Does Vortexia music work 24/7?

Yes. Enable 24/7 mode so Vortexia stays in the voice channel even when the queue is empty or members leave.

Which command starts the music player?

Join a voice channel and run /music to open the player panel, or use /play to queue a track.
