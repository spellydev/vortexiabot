Moderation tools for any staff size

Vortexia gives your team clear slash commands with reasons, so actions are quick and consistent. Lock channels during chaos, purge spam and slow down busy chats without leaving Discord.

Moderation commands

  • /ban and /kick with a reason
  • /timeout with a duration, e.g. /timeout user:@User duration:10m reason:spam
  • /warn for warnings
  • /purge amount:50 to clear messages
  • /lock and /slowmode for channel control

Get started

Add Vortexia Bot, make sure its role sits above the members you want to moderate, then see all options on the commands page.

FAQ

Is the Vortexia moderation bot free?

Yes, moderation commands are part of the free core modules.

What moderation commands does Vortexia have?

Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge, lock and slowmode.

Why can't Vortexia moderate a member?

Discord only lets a bot moderate members whose highest role is below the bot's role, so move Vortexia's role higher in Server Settings.
