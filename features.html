FEATURES

EVERYTHING
YOUR SERVER NEEDS.

Six core modules. Zero paywalls. Built to replace a stack of single-purpose bots.

01 / LAVALINK

Music

Lavalink-powered player with queue, audio filters, lyrics, loop modes and true 24/7 voice presence. No “not a bot” errors — stable streaming for parties and chill channels.

Player panel

One /music opens a polished control panel: play, pause, skip, volume, queue and more.

24/7 mode

Keep Vortexia in the voice channel around the clock so music never drops when people leave.

02 / SAFETY

Moderation

Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge, lock and slowmode — clear slash commands for staff of any size. Fast actions, clean feedback.

Staff tools

/ban /kick /timeout /warn with reasons that land in audit-friendly logs.

Channel control

Lock channels during chaos, purge spam, set slowmode — without leaving Discord.

03 / SUPPORT

Tickets

Button-based ticket panels. Members open private channels for help; staff claim, reply and close — no DM spam.

Setup in seconds

/ticket setup posts a panel. Customize name and let members click to open a ticket.

Staff workflow

Add users, close tickets, keep support organized in dedicated channels.

04 / ENGAGE

Economy & Levels

Server currency, daily claims, pay, leaderboards — plus XP levels from activity. Reward the people who keep the server alive.

Coins

/balance /daily /pay — simple economy that members actually use.

Levels

/rank and leaderboards show progress. Chat more, rank up.

05–06 / CREATE & EVERYDAY

Embeds, dashboard & utility

Build embeds live, open an in-bot dashboard, check user/server info, and handle the small jobs every community needs.

Embed builder

/embed — title, description, color, images. Send polished announcements without external tools.

Dashboard & info

/dashboard /userinfo /serverinfo /help — control and context in one place.
